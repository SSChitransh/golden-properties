Privacy policy

Preview policy for the website build. The business owner must review and adapt this policy to its actual data handling, jurisdiction and service providers before launch.

Draft — requires legal and operational review

1. Information you may provide

The website enquiry forms are designed to ask for basic property-related contact information, such as name, phone number, intent, preferred locality, budget range and an optional message. Do not submit identity documents, bank details or other sensitive information through these forms.

2. Current preview behaviour

In this supplied preview, form submission endpoints are not configured. The website displays a warning and does not send or store submitted details. This behaviour must be re-tested after a secure endpoint is configured.

3. Purpose of use

After launch, information should be used only for responding to the enquiry, coordinating requested contact or visits, and necessary business records. The owner must define the lawful basis, notice and consent language appropriate to the actual operation.

4. Sharing and service providers

The final operator must identify any hosting, form-processing, CRM, email, analytics or messaging providers that may handle data. Do not enable third-party services until their configuration and terms have been reviewed.

5. Retention and security

The business must set a retention period, restrict access to leads, protect systems and credentials, and delete data that no longer needs to be retained. The website source code alone does not provide secure lead storage or a backend.

6. Your choices and contact

Before launch, insert the confirmed privacy contact email and explain how a person can request access, correction or deletion where applicable. An email address is intentionally omitted from this preview because public listings show conflicting addresses.

7. Updates

Update this policy whenever the live form workflow, service providers or data use changes. Have the final version reviewed before publication.

This draft is not legal advice and is not a substitute for a privacy policy tailored to the actual business, hosting environment and applicable law.
